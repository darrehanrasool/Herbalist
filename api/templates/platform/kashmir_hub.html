Automated Ingestion Protocol

4-Stage Kashmir Field Preprocessing

  1. Multi-Format Ingestion: Decodes standard RGB images and 14-bit camera RAW files (.CR2, .CR3, .NEF, .ARW) with camera white balance preservation.
  2. Laplacian Blur & Exposure Audit: Discards motion-blurred captures via 2D discrete Laplacian variance; verifies highlight clipping.
  3. EXIF Altitude Extraction: Decodes GPS altitude metadata; tags specimens across Colline (1400m), Temperate (1800m), Sub-Alpine (2800m), and Alpine Scree (3600m+).
  4. Stratified Train/Val/Test Split: Partitions images by species (70/15/15 ratio), generates data/processed/ directories, and writes audit manifests.
