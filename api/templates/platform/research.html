Peer-Reviewed Methodological Principles

Why Test Accuracy Outweighs Training / Validation Accuracy

IEEE / ACM Best Practice
1. Training Accuracy (Memorization) Measures optimization convergence on images seen by backpropagation. High training accuracy without a held-out test split is prone to over-parameterized memorization.
2. Validation Accuracy (Tuning Filter) Used during development to schedule learning rate decay and trigger EarlyStopping checkpoints. Because hyperparameters are tuned against validation, it carries a mild selection bias.
3. Test Accuracy (True Generalization) The primary metric presented in publication manuscripts. Evaluated on 972 strictly locked, previously unseen test specimens with confirmed 0% data leakage.
